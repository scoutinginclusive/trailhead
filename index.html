Help paying

Cost should never be the reason a Scout or leader stays home. Every council below sets aside money for camp, NYLT or Wood Badge. Ask early: most funds are first come, first served.

Before you apply

Ask your unit first. Many troops and packs cover part of NYLT or Wood Badge from popcorn and camp card sales, and chartered organizations and employers often pay for Wood Badge as leadership training. Apply for camperships before you pay camp fees, since most councils won't refund fees paid before an award.

Training path

Where each course fits, and what has to come before it.

Youth leadership

  1. ILST / ILSCIntroduction to Leadership Skills for Troops or Crews. Run by your own unit, usually after elections. Required before NYLT.
  2. NYLTSix-day council course, ages 13+ and First Class. Offered across the region in winter, spring, summer and fall.
  3. NAYLENational week at the Summit or Philmont for NYLT graduates who hold a unit leadership position.

Adult leaders

  1. Position trainingOnline at my.scouting.org, plus Youth Protection. Required before Wood Badge.
  2. BALOO or IOLSOutdoor skills: BALOO for Cub Scout leaders, IOLS for Scoutmasters and assistants.
  3. Wood BadgeTwo weekends plus a ticket of five goals worked over the following 18 months.

Along the way

  • Order of the ArrowScouting's honor society. Members are elected by their troop; each council has a lodge with its own events.
  • Merit badge eventsWorkshops, merit badge days and colleges. Bring a blue card signed by your Scoutmaster.
  • Fundamentals of TrainingFor youth and adults who will teach ILST or other courses.
