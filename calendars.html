Council calendars

Our Opportunities list highlights training, merit badges, camps and more, but each council's own calendar is the official, complete record. Use these to see everything, including district events, roundtables and things we haven't listed yet.

Tips for council calendars

  • Most councils here use scoutingevent.com. Each page shows a few weeks at a time, so use Next or the page numbers at the bottom to look further ahead.
  • Use the Keyword box to search for things like "BALOO," "merit badge" or "camporee," and the District filter to see your own district.
  • Out-of-council Scouts and leaders are welcome at most training courses and merit badge events. Some charge a small extra fee.
  • Spot something we should list? Let your unit leader or the site's volunteers know, and we'll add it.
