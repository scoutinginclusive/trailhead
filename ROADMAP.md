# Roadmap

Plans for National Pike Trailhead, a Wood Badge ticket project to make Scouting easier to find, join and afford.

## Guiding principles
- **Official sources first.** Link to Scouting America's own published material (scouting.org, council sites) rather than rewriting it, so the official version stays the source of truth and stays current.
- **Check every link every Monday.** Broken, moved or retired links are reported to the owner, not silently removed, so a person decides what to do.
- **Lower the cost of Scouting** for families at both the national and local level.
- **Spread the load.** Every adult takes on a part of Scouting.

## Done
- Opportunities list from 10 councils within about two hours of Laurel, MD (NYLT, ILST/ILSC, NAYLE, Wood Badge, BALOO/IOLS, merit badges, OA, camps).
- Help paying, Councils and Training path tabs.
- Weekly automatic update with a safety check and content standards.
- Weekly link check with a report to the owner.

## Planned: Guides tab
Step-by-step guides built from official Scouting America material, each linking to the source documents.

### 1. Merit badges, focused on Eagle-required badges
- How the merit badge process works for Scouts and parents.
- The current Eagle-required badges, checked against the official list when built (the list changes).
- Where to earn them locally, linked to merit badge events on the site.

### 2. Becoming a merit badge counselor
- Why counselors matter: they are often what keeps Scouts moving toward Eagle.
- The steps: application, required training (including Youth Protection), and council approval.
- Who to contact in each council.

### 3. Making Scouting affordable
- Fundraising approved by Scouting America and the councils, and the unit money-earning approval process, so units stay within the rules.
- National help (fee waivers, national scholarships) and local help (camperships, fee assistance, first-year support).

### 4. Every adult has a role
- Why it matters that every adult takes on a part: units run better, no one burns out, and every family has a stake in the program.
- The kinds of roles available (unit, district, council, merit badge counselor, trainer) and how to get started, from official Scouting America material.

## Planned: Resources section
- Permanent links to resources the team creates, kept separate from dated listings so the weekly update never removes them.
- Each new hosting location (Google Drive, a Google Site, etc.) gets added to the approved-link list in `index.html` and `tools/check_data.py`.
